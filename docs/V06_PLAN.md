# v0.6 plan — application + closest-formalism attack

## Track 1: realistic workload

1. Prepare staged IEEE-CIS transaction/identity data without redistributing raw files.
2. Define evidence stages and explicit acquisition costs.
3. Define a resolution criterion independently of classifier confidence.
4. Compare current-only, acquire-all, fixed EOD, and adaptive EOD.
5. Perform cost and availability sensitivity analyses.
6. Freeze CSV/JSON outputs only after reproducible execution.

## Track 2: formal novelty attack

1. Formalize access-method answerability.
2. Formalize the finite EOD fragment.
3. Attempt translation in both directions.
4. Identify whether EOD is:
   - an equivalent reformulation on the fragment,
   - a conservative extension,
   - incomparable under stated assumptions, or
   - genuinely separated by a counterexample.
5. Repeat against epistemic planning with sensing actions.

## Stop condition

If a closest formalism reproduces EOD's answer/obligation/impossibility semantics without additional structure, narrow or abandon the novelty claim rather than relabeling established theory.
